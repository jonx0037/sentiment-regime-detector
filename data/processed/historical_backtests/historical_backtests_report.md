## Methodology

**Data Sources:**
- Sentiment: PostgreSQL database with 281K+ texts
- VIX: CBOE volatility index (ground truth)
- Coverage: Reddit (WSB, investing), News, Social Media

**Feature Engineering:**
- Cross-asset mean sentiment
- Cross-asset sentiment divergence
- Volume spike (vs 14-day baseline)
- Bearish/Bullish ratio
- Sentiment momentum (3-day, 7-day)

**Classification Rules:**
- High Volatility: Volume spike > 3x baseline
- Elevated: Volume spike > 2x OR divergence > 0.2
- Normal: Baseline levels
- Low Volatility: Positive sentiment + low volume
