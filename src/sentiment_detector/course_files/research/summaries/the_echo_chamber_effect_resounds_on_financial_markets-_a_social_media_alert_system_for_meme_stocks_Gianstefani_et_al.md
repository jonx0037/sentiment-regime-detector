# [The Echo Chamber Effect Resounds on Financial Markets: A Social Media Alert System for Meme Stocks]()

## [[Ilaria Gianstefania]]; [[Luigi Longoa]]; [[and Massimo Riccabonia a IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca]] et al.

## Abstract
The short squeeze of Gamestop (GME) has revealed to the world how retail investors, pooling through social media, can severely impact financial markets. In this paper, we devise an early warning signal to detect suspicious users’ social network activity that might affect financial market stability. We apply our approach to Reddit data, selecting both meme and non-meme stocks as case studies. The alert system is structured in two stages: the first is based on extraordinary activity on the social network, while the second aims to identify whether the movement seeks to coordinate users for a bulk action. We conduct an event study to examine the financial markets' reaction when the alert system detects social network turmoil.

## Key points
- Retail investors have usually been considered noise traders in the financial and market microstructure literature: their choices are not driven by the knowledge of the fundamentals of a stock or any sophisticated analysis of the market, but they are guided primarily by their emotional and irrational beliefs (see [^Black_1986_a]).
- Before delving into the contribution of our paper, we cross-reference all the works dealing with the GameStop case, the triggering factor of this piece of literature devoted to creating an alert system to prevent the dysfunctional social network activity from destabilizing the financial market
- This paper provides empirical evidence of the echo chamber effect on the financial market from the social network, a form of market manipulation
- We design an alert system to detect abnormal movement related to a specific stock on social media based on extraordinary activity in terms of volume and the detection of a potential manipulator that coordinates the mass movement
- While it is far from our duty to evaluate whether the promotion and persuasion practice falls within the boundaries of the law, our consideration concerns the market microstructure models. In front of these episodes, the retail investors can no longer be relegated to a residual category of ’noise traders’, but models should consider that many small and apparently harmless investors, if aggregated and coordinated, can generate a disruptive effect on the financial market
- An additional layer of analysis should focus on applying NLP techniques to the text of Reddit users and to the analysis of data from other social networks


## Summary

### Introduction
The GameStop short squeeze has shown how retail investors, coordinated through social media, can significantly impact financial markets.
This event has highlighted the need for an early warning system to detect suspicious social network activity that may affect market stability.
The paper proposes an alert system to identify potential misconduct on social media that could lead to market manipulation.

### Social Media Impact
Social media has transformed market dynamics, allowing retail investors to access information and coordinate their actions.
The literature review highlights the growing importance of retail traders and the impact of social media on financial markets.
Studies have shown that social media activity can be associated with changes in stock prices and trading volumes.
The paper builds on this research, focusing on the role of social networks in shaping investment strategies and affecting market outcomes.

### Alert System
The proposed alert system has two stages: the first detects unusual activity on social networks, and the second determines whether the movement is seeking to coordinate users for a bulk action.
The system relies on social listening and social network analysis to identify suspicious activity.
The paper aims to contribute to the literature by developing an alert system to detect potential misconduct on social media and prevent harmful coordination that could destabilize financial markets.
The study developed an alert system based on information retrieved from social networks to detect potential misconduct at the social network level.
The alert system consisted of two consecutive stages: the first stage screened days with extensive ticker-related activity, and the second stage monitored the stock until the average number of active indicators over the previous three days returned to normal.
The alert system used volume-related metrics, including the number of submissions, active users, and overall activity on the subreddit, to identify extraordinary ticker-related activity.
The alert system is a two-stage process that detects exceptional activity on social networks, specifically Reddit.
The first stage identifies days with high activity, and the second stage reconstructs the network structure to model interactions among agents.
The system detects users who act like leaders, gain trust and popularity, and identifies the top authors with the highest relative number of incoming links.

### Data Collection
The study collected data from the subreddit r/WallStreetBets and other related subreddits, including r/WallStreetBetsnew, r/GME, and r/AMC, from October 2020 to April 2022.
The data included posts and comments containing the tickers of six stocks: GME, AMC, TSLA, NOK, AAPL, and MSFT.
The study used the Python Reddit API Wrapper (PRAW) to download data and extract the relevant comment forests and attributes.

### Methodology
The study employed a two-step analysis to identify potential users who might trigger collective action in financial markets.
The first step involved identifying days with high levels of social media activity, while the second aimed to detect potential influential users who were leading the community toward a particular action.
The study used a network analysis approach to reconstruct the daily agent interactions from raw tree-level data and defined a network in which nodes represented active users and edges represented users' interactions through comments.

### Abnormal Returns
Abnormal returns are calculated using a market model to assess the impact of detected events on the financial market.
The results show significant positive abnormal returns around the alert day, with higher abnormal returns the day after.
The average abnormal return presents higher volatility after the event, with a significant positive impact the day after.

### Event Detection
The alert system is applied to six tickers: four meme stocks (GME, AMC, TSLA, NOK) and two controls (AAPL, MSFT).
The system identifies 49 days with suspicious movements on the social network for GME, 10 for AMC, 12 for TSLA, and 4 for NOK, while only 2 and 1 for AAPL and MSFT, respectively.
The results show that the alert system can filter out noise from high-volume social activity and user coordination and identify the few users who drive the movement.

### Event Analysis
The event analysis suggests significant abnormal returns following the alert dates for the meme stock.
The histogram for a stock at h = 0 is 0.5, indicating that half of the abnormal returns are positive and half are negative.
The plot shows an upward trend in the rate of positive abnormal returns from days before the event to days after.

### Social Media Influence
Social media is a powerful tool for disseminating information and reaching a vast audience.
Influential social media users, or "fanatics," can coordinate many naive investors to provoke the desired stock price movement.
The analysis provides empirical evidence of the echo chamber effect on the financial market from social networks, a form of market manipulation.

### Market Implications
The detection system generates alerts for both meme and non-meme stocks, but the non-meme stocks never show abnormal returns, suggesting a lower likelihood of suspicious trading activity or market manipulation in those cases.
The analysis identifies significant differences between meme and non-meme stocks, with evidence of positive average abnormal returns around alert days and high, persistent volatility.

## Contributions

Reddit and, in general, social media are great places to share advice and manipulate poorly informed, unsophisticated, and easily convinced investors. <mark class="claim">We provide evidence that influential social media users, or to use a word a la Pedersen, the fanatics, can coordinate many naive investors to provoke the desired stock price movement</mark>. The fanatics can effectively undermine the financial market stability by persuading inexperienced and easily reachable people. <mark class="fact">We design an alert system to detect abnormal movement related to a specific stock on social media based on extraordinary activity</mark> in terms of volume and the detection of a potential manipulator that coordinates the mass movement. While it is <mark class="fact">far from our duty to evaluate whether the promotion and persuasion practice falls within the boundaries</mark> of the law, our consideration concerns the market microstructure models. In front of these episodes, <mark class="fact">the retail investors can no longer be relegated to a residual category</mark> of ’noise traders’, but models should consider that many small and apparently harmless investors, if aggregated and coordinated, can generate a disruptive effect on the financial market. In the end, the analysis identifies significant differences between meme and non-meme stocks. The detection system finds alerts for both categories. Still, <mark class="fact">they never yield abnormal returns for non-meme stocks</mark>, suggesting a lower likelihood of suspicious trading activity or market manipulation in those cases. Moreover, <mark class="claim">we find evidence of positive significant average abnormal returns around the days of the alert and generally high and persistent volatility, especially the days</mark>. For this reason, it is crucial to continually update monitoring techniques to evaluate potential breaks in the behavior of social network agents.

## Limitations
- The study notes that the literature on social network evolution and its impact on financial markets is still in its infancy. The paper also acknowledges that few works address this issue.
- The study has several limitations, including the use of a limited dataset and the focus on a specific subreddit. The study also notes that the reduction in user participation on the WallStreetBets subreddit after June 2021 may have affected the results.
- The study has limitations, including the fact that the threshold numbers are not tuned in an in/out-of-sample analysis, and that the results are based on a limited number of time observations.
- The study notes that the effectiveness of the alert system may be compromised by changes in user behavior on social media. The study also notes that the analysis is limited to a specific dataset and time period.

## Future work
- The study suggests that future research should focus on testing model predictions using data on networks and market behavior. The paper also notes that the availability of social media data can open up new research possibilities.
- The study suggests that future research could focus on tuning the model to produce better performance in identifying future alerts, and on analyzing the impact of the detected events on the trading volume.
- The study suggests that future work should focus on backtesting the alert system using more data and tuning the parameters of the algorithm. The study also suggests that future work should apply natural language processing (NLP) techniques to Reddit users' text and analyze data from other social networks.
